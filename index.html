FAQ

FAQ

What is Influencer Marketing?

Influencer marketing leverages the power of social media personalities to promote products and services for brands/companies. Influencers span across virtually every niche, making it possible to find the perfect match for any brand or industry.

Why Influencer Marketing?

Influencer marketing has quickly become the go-to strategy for marketers, offering an innovative and highly effective solution to modern marketing needs. With businesses earning an estimated $5 for every $1 spent, it's clear that consumers are much more inclined to trust recommendations from influencers.

Must I have a certain amount of following to join the network?

We work with all content creators of all sizes. Whether you have 1,000 followers or 1M followers, we are here to work with you and find you the best deal possible. Connect with us to see if you would be a good fit!

What's the catch?

For influencers, none! We simply connect influencers with brands that are interested in working with their type of personality/content. Joining our network is non-exclusive so there are no downside for the influencers. They are free to leave the network whenever they want! Joining the network is FREE.

For brands, none! We simply gather a massive database that allows the brand to see what kind of influencers we have in our system and see whether or not the content creators would be a good fit.
